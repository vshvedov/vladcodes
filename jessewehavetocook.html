Jesse, We Have to Cook - Mac Menu Bar Utility

Keep your Mac awake and stay active in Slack, Telegram, Microsoft Teams, Discord, and other messaging applications. Jesse, We Have to Cook is a free, lightweight menu bar utility for macOS that prevents your computer from going to sleep, ensuring you remain active in work communication platforms.

Perfect for remote work, work from home scenarios, and maintaining presence in team collaboration tools. This productivity app runs silently in your menu bar with minimal system impact.

System Requirements

  • Operating System: macOS 10.14 (Mojave) or later
  • Architecture: Intel and Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3) Macs supported
  • Memory: ~35MB RAM usage
  • Storage: Under 4MB disk space

Installation Instructions

  1. Download the JWHTC-v.1.1.zip file
  2. Unzip the downloaded file
  3. Drag the app to your Applications folder (optional)
  4. Double-click to run - the app will appear in your menu bar
  5. Click the menu bar icon to start keeping your Mac awake

Jesse, We Have to Cook is compatible with all modern macOS versions. No administrator privileges required. The app is ad-hoc code-signed for security.
