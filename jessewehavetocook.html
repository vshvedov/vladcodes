Jesse, We Have to Cook

Keep your Mac awake and stay active in Slack, Teams, and other apps. A simple menu bar utility inspired by everyone's favorite chemistry teacher.

Features

  • Simple & Minimalistic: Clean, intuitive interface that stays out of your way
  • Lightweight: Low RAM and CPU footprint - won't slow down your system
  • Open Source: Fully transparent codebase available on GitHub
  • Free: No cost, no subscriptions, no hidden fees
  • Menu Bar Utility: Convenient access right from your menu bar
