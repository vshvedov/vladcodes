Jesse, We Have to Cook

A macOS utility to prevent sleep and stay active in messaging apps

Keep your Mac awake and stay active while compiling, streaming or thinking. Jesse, We Have to Cook is a free, lightweight menu bar utility for macOS that prevents your computer from going to sleep, ensuring you remain active in work communication platforms.

Installation

To install "Jesse, We Have to Cook", simply download the latest release from the link above and unzip the file. Drag the application to your Applications folder and launch it.

⚠️ Note: As this is a self-signed app distributed outside of the Mac App Store, you may need to right-click the app and select "Open" the first time you run it to bypass macOS security warnings. Or go to System Preferences > Security & Privacy > General and click "Open Anyway" for the app.

Alternatively, you can compile the app from source if you have Xcode installed. Clone the repository, open the Xcode project, and build & run the app on your machine.
